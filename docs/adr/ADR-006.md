# ADR-006 — Recovery & rollback policy (restore first, diagnose second)

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-07

## Context
A broken operating cycle, a stuck lock, or a half-written trace must be
addressed by a normal operator without fresh architecture decisions. Without a
discipline, recovery becomes improvisation under pressure — exactly when it
fails.

## Decision
Recovery procedures live in `docs/operations/Recovery.md` with a strict
timeline (clear lock → confirm data → clean build → post-mortem in
`docs/DecisionLog.md`). The rollback executor (`orchestrator`, ADR-004) is the
primary failover for engine instability. Broken or partial traces are never
committed as good runs. Any policy/payload change during recovery is recorded
in the decision log.

## Consequences
- Recovery is documented, time-boxed, and tested.
- **Positive:** a broken system is restored to a known-good state deterministically.
- **Negative:** the checklist gate must actually be followed; a skipped check
  reintroduces risk (see SafetyGuardrails).

Related: ADR-004, ADR-005.
